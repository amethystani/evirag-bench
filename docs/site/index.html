IIPipeline

INTENTResolved or contested.
RETRIEVEFour roles: precision, recall, skeptic, counterfactual.
CLAIMSAtomic claims tied to passages.
PAIRSSupport, contradict or neutral, with a CDA-7 cause.
PARTITIONSigned Louvain over the claim graph.
TIMEDated evidence per year.
VIEWSPosition, evidence, weaknesses, sources, confidence.
