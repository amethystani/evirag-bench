# CDA-7 labeling guide

Label a claim pair as contradictory only when both claims concern comparable propositions and cannot both hold under the stated conditions. Then choose the main reason for the difference:

1. **Replication:** A later attempt repeats the earlier design and fails to recover its finding.
2. **Population:** Samples, age groups, settings, or inclusion criteria differ.
3. **Operational:** The studies use different definitions or outcome measures for the same named concept.
4. **Methodological:** The study design, intervention, protocol, or control strategy differs.
5. **Statistical:** Estimates or interpretations of uncertainty differ despite comparable designs.
6. **Temporal:** Evidence from a later period differs from evidence from an earlier period.
7. **Theoretical:** Competing causal or explanatory frameworks account for the same observations.

If more than one cause applies, store the primary cause on the edge and explain secondary causes in the view summary. Keep the source passage IDs with both claims.
